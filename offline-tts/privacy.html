Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version: OwnVoice collects nothing, and there is nothing to collect.

  • The app makes no network requests. It never connects to a server, because there is no server.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Your recording, the personal voice built from it, and every audio file you generate stay on this device.
  • You can delete any of it from inside the app, at any time.

1. What we collect

Nothing. OwnVoice has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party component that gathers data. It contains no networking code at all, so it is not merely a promise that your data is not sent somewhere — the app has no way to send it.

We do not have a database of users, because we have no users to record: no email address, no name, no device identifier and no usage history ever reaches us.

2. Microphone and your voice

OwnVoice asks for microphone access only when you tap Record my voice, and only to record the short passage the app reads with you. Before the first recording it asks you to confirm that the voice is your own.

  • The recording is used to build a personal voice, on this device.
  • Both the recording and the personal voice built from it are stored in the app's own private storage, protected by iOS file encryption.
  • They are not exported, not backed up to iCloud, not shared and not uploaded. The app does not enable iCloud storage for them.
  • You can delete the personal voice at any time from the voice list, and the app will ask you to record again before it can be used.

If you deny microphone access, the app keeps working normally with the voices built into iOS; only the personal voice feature is unavailable.

3. Text you enter

Text you type or paste is held in memory only while the audio is being produced, and is not stored anywhere by the app. It is never transmitted, because nothing is transmitted. If you close the app, the text is gone.

4. Audio you generate

Generated audio is saved as a WAV file inside the app's private storage on this device, so that you can replay it later. It stays there until you delete it, and you are always in control of it.

How to remove it:

To removeWhere

Everything at onceDelete the app from your device; iOS removes its storage with it

5. Sharing and exporting

When you tap Share, iOS presents the standard share sheet and hands the file to whichever app you pick. That is a deliberate action by you: the file leaves the app's storage because you asked it to, and what happens to it afterwards is governed by the privacy policy of the app or service you chose, not by this one.

If you never use Share, no file produced by OwnVoice ever leaves this device.

6. Purchases

OwnVoice is sold once through the App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details, your Apple Account or any part of your billing information. The app itself contains no in-app purchases and no subscription, so there is nothing further to transact inside it.

7. Requests from the app

The app asks for only two things, and both are optional:

  • Microphone — only when you start a personal voice recording.
  • Sharing — only when you tap a share button.

Nothing else is requested: no notifications, no tracking permission, no contacts, no photos. If you ever want to review the microphone decision, open iOS Settings, find OwnVoice, and change it there.

8. Your own voice only

The personal voice feature exists to read text in your voice. OwnVoice ships with no celebrity voices and no third-party voices of any kind, and it asks you to confirm that the recording is your own before it starts.

Please do not use this app to imitate another person, and do not use audio it produces to mislead anyone. You are responsible for how you use the audio you create.

9. Children

OwnVoice does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, because it does not collect information at all. There is no account to create and no content to submit, so there is nothing for a child to disclose to us.

10. Your rights

Privacy regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data held about you — to access it, correct it, delete it, or be told what is held. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, amend or erase on your behalf. Everything the app stores is already under your direct control on your own device, and the app's own delete options are the complete set of data controls.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect changes that affect your privacy — but the date will tell you when the text was last reviewed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in OwnVoice generally? Email supportbx@163.com and we will answer.
