Three steps, then listen

  1. Choose a voice

    The voices iOS already has are listed for you, with the ones for your language first. Tap Preview on any of them to hear it read your text before you decide.

  2. Paste your text

    An article, a chapter, meeting notes, a script. Length is not a problem — long passages are split by sentence and joined back into a single file.

  3. Tap Generate

    The audio is created on the device, saved to My Audio and played straight away. Keep it, rename it, share it, or send it to the Files app.

Why people choose OwnVoice

Genuinely offline

No network connection is ever opened. Airplane mode is not a workaround here — it is the normal way to use it. Nothing you type or record can leak, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Read it in your own voice

Record about ten seconds of speech and OwnVoice builds a personal voice from it, on the device. It then sits in the voice list beside the built-in ones and reads anything you type.

One purchase, yours forever

No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no watermark, no upsells. Every feature is included from the moment you buy it.

Voices that are already there

OwnVoice reads with the voices built into iOS, so they work instantly and without a download. Novelty sound effects are filtered out — you get reading voices, not sound effects.

Long text handled for you

Paste as much as you like. The app splits it by sentence, generates each part and joins the result into one WAV file — no manual chopping.

Audio you actually keep

Every generation is saved to My Audio, where you can play, rename, share or delete it, and export it through the standard iOS share sheet.

Details, without the clutter

Speed, pitch and tone live in a folded panel called Reading details, closed by default. The defaults are chosen to sound right, so most people never open it — but when you want a slower read for a long document or a livelier one for a script, it is one tap away. A single Reset puts everything back.

Your voice stays on your phone

This is the whole point of the app, so it is worth being precise about it. OwnVoice contains no networking code at all: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no third-party data collection. Your recording, the personal voice built from it, and every audio file you generate are kept in the app's private storage on this device, protected by iOS encryption — not exported, not backed up to iCloud, not uploaded.

You can delete any single clip, clear all generated audio, or erase everything including your personal voice, from inside the app. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Please only clone your own voice

OwnVoice is for reading text in your own voice. It asks you to confirm that before your first recording, and it ships with no celebrity or third-party voices of any kind.

Who it is for

People who would rather listen than read: students and commuters working through long documents, language learners, writers checking how a sentence sounds out loud, and anyone who has ever wished their phone could read to them without sending the text to somebody else's computer.
