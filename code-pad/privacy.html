Privacy Policy

CodePad++ for iOS · Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: CodePad++ is a fully offline app. It makes no network requests, collects no personal information, and uploads nothing — your files never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

CodePad++ does not collect any personal information. The app embeds no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, push notifications or tracking, and it never reads your contacts, photos, location, device identifiers or any other system data.

2. Network Access

CodePad++ is a fully offline application. During normal use it makes no network requests of any kind and does not transmit any data to any server. There is no account system, no sign-in and no cloud service.

3. Local Data

Files you open are copied into the app's private sandbox on your device so they can be listed under "Recent". These copies exist only on your device:

• Removing an entry, or using "Clear entries" in Settings, deletes the corresponding local copy permanently.

• Uninstalling the app removes all local copies together with the app itself.

CodePad++ never reads the contents of your files for any purpose other than displaying them to you on screen.

4. Permissions

CodePad++ requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts. File access happens exclusively through the iOS document picker and the system "Open in CodePad++" action, which you initiate.

5. Email Feedback

The app offers an optional feedback form that opens your own email client via the system Mail app. If you choose to send feedback, the content of that email — including anything you voluntarily include — is handled by your email provider under their privacy policy. CodePad++ receives and processes your email only to respond to your request.

6. Children's Privacy

CodePad++ is a professional tool not directed to children, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone — including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after a revision constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or the app's privacy practices, contact us at supportbx@163.com or through the in-app feedback form.
