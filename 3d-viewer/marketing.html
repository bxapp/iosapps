Questions & answers

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Is View3D Pro really free of internet?

Yes. All rendering happens locally on your device. No files are ever uploaded or synced.

Can it open large models?

Absolutely. Files up to 100MB are supported. Very large files show a quick confirmation before opening.

How do I get my files into the app?

Open any STL, OBJ, GLB, or GLTF directly from Files, AirDrop, Mail, or any sharing app.

Is my data safe?

Your files stay in the app's private sandbox and are completely removed when you uninstall the app.
