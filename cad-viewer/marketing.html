DWG & DXF, open in seconds.

A fast, professional and completely offline CAD drawing viewer for iPhone and iPad.

Fast. Drawings are parsed and rendered locally by a built-in CAD engine — no upload, no conversion, no waiting on a server.

Offline and private. Your drawings never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server — a safe choice for confidential or client work.

One purchase. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no recurring charges. Every feature is included.

Open drawings from anywhere

Tap Share or Open in in Mail, Files, a chat app or your cloud drive and choose ViewCAD Pro — the app goes straight to the drawing. You can also tap Import Drawing on the home screen to browse for a file, and the recent list brings you back to a drawing in one tap.

Built for real drawings

DWG and DXF

Both major CAD formats, as used across engineering, architecture and manufacturing.

Layer control

Every layer listed with its original colour. Tap to hide or show — the drawing updates instantly.

Dark and light canvas

Switch in one tap. Dark for site work, light for checking against screens or paper.

Smooth navigation

Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-tap to fit. The toolbar fades away so the drawing fills the screen.

Screenshot and share

Save the current view to your photo library, or send the original drawing file to colleagues.

Landscape and portrait

Designed for iPhone and iPad, in either orientation, with a wide landscape view for large sheets.

Your drawings stay yours

ViewCAD Pro has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Parsing and rendering happen entirely on your device, and the app works with no network connection at all — on a plane, in a basement, on a remote site.

Try it before you import

Three sample drawings are included so you can explore the app right away: a mechanical flange, an architectural floor plan and an electrical schematic.

In eleven languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian and Italian. The interface follows your system theme, or you can set light and dark manually.

Good to know

  • ViewCAD Pro is a viewer: it does not modify, edit, measure or convert drawings.
  • Very large files can take a moment to open on older devices.
  • No account or personal information is required to use the app.
