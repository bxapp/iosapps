In App Store terms, ViewVector's privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. How the app handles your files

When you open a file, ViewVector reads it on your device and renders it on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app makes no network requests.

  • Import — the file you pick is copied into the app's private storage on this device, so it can be reopened from the Recents list.
  • Processing — parsing, rendering, measurement, bleed preview and export all happen locally using components bundled inside the app.
  • Exports — PNG and PDF files you export are created locally, then handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where they go next is entirely your choice.
  • Removal — touch and hold an entry in Recents, or use Clear Recents in Settings, to delete the entry and its local copy. Deleting an entry never affects the original file in Files, iCloud, Dropbox or wherever you opened it from.

Removing the app from your device deletes all of its local storage, including the Recents list and every imported copy.

2. No network access

ViewVector is built without network capability. There are no servers, no APIs, no cloud processing, no over-the-air updates, no account systems and no telemetry. Because there is no network access, the app cannot transmit your files or any information about you — even in the event of a software defect.

3. Purchases

ViewVector is sold as a one-time purchase through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple Account. ViewVector has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

4. Children's privacy

ViewVector does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app contains no advertising, no user-generated content, no social features and no external links that gather information.

5. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data, because we do not collect any. There are no third-party service providers receiving data from this app.

6. Optional email support

If you choose to email us at supportbx@163.com, we receive your email address, your message and any attachment you decide to send. We use it only to answer your question and to fix the issue. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not share it with anyone. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

7. Format compatibility

For transparency, these are the limits of the preview engine:

  • AI — only PDF-compatible Illustrator files (AI 9 and newer). Legacy native AI files and exclusive effects may be simplified or unavailable.
  • DXF — 2D drawings only. 3D entities and advanced styles are simplified.
  • SVG / PDF — standard preview. Advanced professional effects may be simplified; scripts and embedded interactive content are not executed.

ViewVector is a preview and preflight tool. Always verify production files in your original software before printing, cutting or engraving.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Because ViewVector collects no data, future changes are expected to be clarifications only.
