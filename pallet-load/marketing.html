Built for people who load containers for a living

Every day, someone is asked the same two questions: how many boxes fit on this pallet, and how many pallets fit in this container? Pallet Master answers both before the truck arrives — and shows you the answer in 3D.

100% offline and private

No internet, no account, no cloud sync, no analytics. Every standard, the algorithm and the 3D engine run on your device — and your data never leaves it.

A professional stacking engine

Tests both orientations and both patterns — column and 90° interlock — then recommends the plan with the highest box count and the best stability, with 2–3 alternatives to compare.

See it before you load it

Orbit, zoom and pan the load in 3D. Snap to top, 45° or side view. Hide any layer to check how each course is arranged, then save a screenshot to your photo library.

Standards already loaded

20GP, 40GP, 40HQ, 45HQ containers and 4.2 m / 9.6 m trucks. Eight pallet standards from EUR to CHEP and US 48×40 in. Or enter any custom size, tare weight and capacity.

Paperwork in one tap

Export a PDF worksheet with the full parameter table and the embedded 3D preview — then share, email or print it for the loading team.

Two taps to a plan

Ninety per cent of jobs need only the basics. Every advanced constraint — max layers, height cap, forklift clearance, rotation — stays folded away until you need it.

What you get on the result screen

No spreadsheets, no guessing, no phone calls from the dock.

Key figures, instantly

  • Boxes per layer, number of layers and boxes per pallet
  • Pallet stacking height and total gross weight
  • Pallet space utilisation
  • Pallets per carrier and boxes per carrier
  • Clear red warnings for over-height and over-weight loads

Planning that scales

  • Capacity mode — how much fits on one pallet and in one carrier
  • Quantity mode — enter the shipment quantity to get the exact number of pallets and carriers, plus what rides on the last partial pallet
  • History — save any plan with a thumbnail and reopen it with every parameter restored
  • Light and dark appearance, iPhone and iPad, 12 languages
