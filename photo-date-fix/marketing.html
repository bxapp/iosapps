Your originals are protected by design

Editing photos should never put your library at risk.

  • Originals are never modified. Every processed photo is saved as a new, separate copy.
  • Every write is verified. The new timestamp is read back and compared before the copy is saved. If it does not match exactly, the output is discarded and reported as failed — you never get a silently corrupted duplicate.
  • Cancel any time. Finished copies are kept, and photos that had not been processed yet stay completely untouched.
  • Nothing is a mystery. The final report shows how many photos were saved, skipped and failed, with a plain reason for each one.

Good to know before you buy

We would rather explain iOS's limits up front than surprise you later. This is exactly what the app can and cannot do.

  • Only the EXIF capture timestamp changesiOS does not allow any app to rewrite a photo's file-system creation date.
  • Output is always a new copyYour original file stays exactly as it was.
  • JPEG and HEIC are supportedPNG, GIF, ProRAW and DNG are not.
  • Live Photos are partially supportedOnly the still image's metadata is updated; the paired video is not included in the copy.
  • iCloud-only photos must be downloaded firstProcessing happens on your device, so the file has to be local.
  • EXIF timestamps carry no time zoneThe app adjusts the absolute time value you specify.
