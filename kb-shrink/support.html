Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a photo to an exact size?

Open KB Shrink and tap Select Photo. Choose a preset — such as US Passport or Visa Application — or switch to Custom Size and type the limit in KB. KB Shrink automatically finds the highest quality that fits, then shows the result. Tap Save to Photos or Share.

Can I choose the size the website requires?

Yes. Any target from 10 KB up to 10 MB. Type the value in KB (for example 200) and the app compresses the photo to that size or slightly below it.

The compressed photo looks a little soft. Why?

Very small targets — below roughly 50 KB — cannot hold much detail, especially in photos with text or fine patterns. That is a limit of image compression itself: the smaller the file, the more detail is discarded. When a limit is extremely low, KB Shrink also reduces the pixel dimensions so the result stays as clean as possible.

Which formats are supported?

You can compress JPG, JPEG, PNG and HEIC photos from your photo library, iCloud Drive or the Files app. Results are saved as JPEG, the format accepted by virtually every upload form.

Can I compress a photo without opening the app?

Yes. Open the photo in Safari, Mail or any other app, tap Share and choose KB Shrink. The app opens directly in the size picker, so you can compress and return to what you were doing.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. KB Shrink processes everything on your iPhone and makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no cloud storage and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for details.

Where are my compressed photos stored?

Photos you save go to your own photo library. KB Shrink also keeps the most recent results inside the app so you can revisit or re-share them; you can remove them at any time with Settings → Clear App Records.

Why does the app ask for photo access?

Only to add your compressed photo to your library, and only when you tap Save. KB Shrink requests the limited, add-only permission — it never reads or scans your library. Selecting a photo uses the system picker, which needs no permission at all.

Is there a subscription?

No. KB Shrink is a one-time purchase: unlimited compressions, every preset and every language are included. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and select the purchase.
