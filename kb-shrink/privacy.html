1. Who we are

KB Shrink is an independent iPhone and iPad application that compresses photos to a chosen file size. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It applies to the app itself and to this website.

For any privacy question, contact us at supportbx@163.com.

2. Data we collect

None. KB Shrink has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server backend and no network capability. We cannot see your photos, your file names, your device identifiers or your usage.

3. How your photos are handled

  • Selection. When you pick a photo, iOS shows the system photo picker. KB Shrink only receives the single photo you choose.
  • Processing. Compression happens entirely inside the app's own sandbox on your device, using the operating system's image framework.
  • Metadata. The image is re-encoded when compressed, which permanently removes hidden EXIF data such as GPS location, device model and capture time.
  • Saving. If you tap “Save to Photos”, iOS asks for add-only photo library permission. KB Shrink can only add the item you created; it cannot read or browse your library.
  • On-device history. The most recent results are kept inside the app so you can revisit or re-share them. They stay on your device and can be deleted at any time with Settings → Clear App Records.

4. Network activity

KB Shrink makes no network requests. There is no server to send data to, no cloud processing and no remote configuration. The app is fully functional with no internet connection.

5. Third parties

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs. If you choose to contact us by email, your message is sent through your own email provider and is used solely to answer your request.

6. Children

KB Shrink is a general-purpose utility suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights under the GDPR and CCPA

Privacy regulations such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to opt out of its sale.

Since KB Shrink collects no personal data and sells no data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, delete or transfer. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not process personal data for advertising. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. International data transfers

There are none, because no data leaves your device.

9. Security

Your photos are protected by the iOS sandbox and the device's own protections, including device passcode and encryption. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side data that could be exposed in a breach.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's App Store release notes.

11. Contact
