The six layers

Each layer answers one question — and they are mutually exclusive, so the picture never turns into a pile of effects.

Value MapGrayscale posterized into 3–9 steps: is the value structure readable?
NotanContrast pushed, then reduced to 2–4 flat tones: does the light–dark design hold?
MassesNeighbouring colors merged into large shapes: how do the big blocks relate?
Warm–CoolThe picture tinted from blue to red: where does the temperature turn?
ContoursEdge extraction to black lines on white: where does the drawing actually go?
Color MapThe image repainted with its 3–6 dominant colors, with proportions listed.
