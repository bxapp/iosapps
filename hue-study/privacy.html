Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Applies to HueStudy for iPhone and iPad (version 1.0.0 and later)

HueStudy is an offline study tool for painters. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. The short version: it stays on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your images or your work, and the app does not ask you to create an account.

Data we collectNone.
AccountsNone. There is no sign-up, sign-in or profile.
Analytics, advertising, trackingNone. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.
Network requestsNone made by the app. Importing, analysis, pigment mixing and exporting all run on your device.
Where your work is storedIn the app's own storage on your device only.
Data sold or sharedNone — there is nothing collected to sell or share.

1. Information the app accesses, and why

HueStudy asks for permissions only when a feature needs them. Each one is used on your device, for that feature, and nothing is transmitted off the device.

  • Photos (read) — to import the reference image you choose from your library.
  • Photos (add) — to save a study you exported back to your photo library.
  • Camera — optional; used when you photograph a swatch you mixed, to compare it with the color you sampled.
  • Files — to import an image from the Files app.

Images you import, camera frames you capture, sample points, grids, layer settings, pigment recipes and exports are processed and stored locally in the app's container on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Projects you create: the imported image, its analysis state (sample points, grid, layer settings) and a thumbnail.
  • Preferences: interface language, appearance, default sampling radius.

You can delete a single project inside the app, clear all projects in Settings, or remove everything at once by deleting the app.

3. Email you send us

The in-app feedback option opens your own mail app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message — used only to answer you and to improve the app, never sold or added to a mailing list. Email we receive through supportbx@163.com is kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation.

4. Purchases

HueStudy is a paid app, and the purchase is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or billing address. Apple's handling of that transaction is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. The app itself contains no in-app purchases.

5. Children

HueStudy collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is no user-generated content sharing, no messaging, no advertising and no way to contact other users through the app.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on your behalf. Data held by the app lives in the app's storage on your device and is under your control: delete projects in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. If you have written to us and would like that correspondence deleted, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Your work never leaves your device, which removes the usual risks of transmission and server storage. The app's data is protected by your device's own protections (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS file encryption).

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated here before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in HueStudy generally:

Email: supportbx@163.com

We aim to reply within a few days. Please include the app version shown at the top of Settings.
