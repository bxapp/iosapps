HueStudy is a study tool for painters: import a reference photo, sample colors precisely, read the picture as values and shapes, and get a pigment mixing recipe for what you see — all offline, on your device.

Getting started in five steps

  1. On the home screen, tap Import Image (Photos, Files, or camera).
  2. Tap the picture to drop a sample point. Long-press to bring up the loupe when you need precision; pinch to zoom, drag to move.
  3. Open Color to read HEX, RGB, HSV, LAB, value and saturation; open Display for the grid.
  4. Open Layers for the six analysis views: Value Map, Notan, Masses, Warm–Cool, Contours, Color Map. Press and hold Original to compare with the untouched photo.
  5. Tap Save to Photos to export your study as a PNG (grid, layers and marks included).

Frequently asked questions

Does HueStudy need an internet connection?

No. It has no accounts, no sync and no server, and it does not make network requests. Everything — importing, analysis, mixing, exporting — runs on your device, so it works the same in a gallery, a classroom or anywhere without signal.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

Where are my photos and projects stored?

Only inside the app's own storage on your device. Deleting a project in the app deletes its image and its analysis data; deleting the app removes everything. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Why does the app ask for photo access?

To import the reference image you want to study, and to save an exported study back to your library. Only the images you pick are used, and only on your device. If you never import from Photos, the app still works.

Why does it ask for camera access?

The camera is used for one optional feature: photograph a swatch you actually mixed and compare it with the color you sampled from the reference. Without camera access, the rest of the app works normally.

The color on screen looks different from my painting. Why?

A screen is not a painting surface: display calibration, ambient light and the sheen of wet paint all shift what you see. Treat the numbers as the reliable part — LAB values and ΔE differences — and confirm the final mix on your own surface in your own light.

Is there a subscription or an in-app purchase?

No. HueStudy is a one-time purchase: every feature is included, and there is no subscription, no consumable and no upgrade prompt.

How many projects and sample points can I keep?

Up to 50 saved projects, and up to 20 sample points on a single image. Comparison works with 2–4 points at a time.

Does it run on iPad?

Yes. HueStudy runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or later, in portrait, in light and dark appearance.

Which languages are available?

The interface is available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian and Italian. Text follows your system language by default, and you can choose a language in Settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple as well: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and select the purchase.

I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.

Please email us with your device model, iOS version, the app version (shown at the top of Settings) and, if possible, a screenshot. Reports go straight to the people who build the app.
