02Getting started

How do I import a log file?

Two ways: (1) In the Files app or any app with a share sheet, tap Share → LogLens++ — the log opens directly. (2) In LogLens++, tap Import log and pick a file. Supported formats: .log .txt .json .gz .zip .tar.gz .rar.

What happens after import?

LogLens++ decompresses .gz automatically, detects the format (log4j-style, JSON lines, syslog, plain text), classifies every line by severity, and indexes it. The reader opens on the first ERROR so you can start reading while indexing continues.

How do I read errors efficiently?

Use the ◀ ERR / ERR ▶ bar to step through errors, the rail on the right to jump anywhere, and tap an error line to expand its context. Long-press a line to copy it, bookmark it, or trace an ID (traceId, request ID, UUID) across the whole file.

03Troubleshooting

The .gz file fails to open

The archive may be damaged, or it decompresses to more than 256 MB. Try unarchiving on your computer and importing the plain log. Splitting very large logs is also a good workaround.

Lines are classified incorrectly

Level detection uses editable regular expressions. Go to Settings → Parsing → Parsing rules to adjust them for your log format, then re-index. You can restore defaults anytime.

A file shows “Indexing failed” or is stuck

Swipe left on the file card and choose Re-index. If the original archive was cleared by iOS, re-import the file. “Re-index” also applies new parsing rules to already-opened files.

Where is my data stored? Can I recover it?

All logs and indexes live only inside the app's sandbox on your device — nothing is synced or uploaded. Deleting a file in LogLens++ (or uninstalling the app) permanently removes it. Keep your source files if you need backups.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

LogLens++ is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. Install the app on the new device and restore purchases from the App Store, or sign in with the same Apple ID — no re-purchase needed.
