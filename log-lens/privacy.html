Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Applies to the LogLens++ iOS app

Short version: LogLens++ collects nothing. It works entirely offline, stores everything on your device, has no account, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind. This page explains exactly what that means.

1Information we collect

None. LogLens++ does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect or process:

  • Log files or their contents
  • Names, email addresses, or identifiers
  • Device information or location
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers

2How LogLens++ works with your data

Log files that you import are copied into the app's private sandbox on your device and are indexed locally. All processing — decompression, format detection, classification, search, and export — happens entirely on your device.

  • Storage: imported logs and their indexes live only in the app sandbox (local database and settings).
  • Deletion: deleting a file in LogLens++ permanently erases it and its index. Uninstalling the app removes everything, with no copies remaining anywhere.
  • Backups: if your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, Apple's standard device-backup rules apply to app data. We never access backups.

3Network access

LogLens++ is built without any networking code. The app does not connect to servers, load remote content, or make external requests of any kind. Import and export use the system share sheet and document picker, which are handled by iOS — the app itself never initiates a connection. You can verify this with any network-monitoring tool: the app is silent.

4Third parties

LogLens++ embeds no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or trackers. There is no one for us (or anyone else) to share your data with, because no data ever reaches us.

5Permissions

LogLens++ requests no special iOS permissions — no photos, camera, microphone, location, or contacts. File import uses the standard iOS document picker, and file export uses the standard iOS share sheet; you choose what to give access to, and only for that action.

6Children

LogLens++ is a developer tool not directed at children under 13, and — since no data is collected from anyone — no data is collected from children.

7Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data that is collected about you. Because LogLens++ collects no personal data and transmits nothing to us, there is no data for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf — you already hold complete control on your device. Purchase and payment information is handled solely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we never see it.

8Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above — and the app will still collect nothing unless it asks you first and says so plainly.

9Contact

Questions about this policy? Email supportbx@163.com or use the in-app support page.
